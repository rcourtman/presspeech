Presspeech vs Wispr Flow

The main difference is where transcription happens and how broad the product is. Wispr Flow transcribes in the cloud, adds AI formatting and meeting notes, and runs across Mac, Windows, and mobile on free and subscription tiers. Presspeech transcribes on-device, applies only deterministic text handling, and is free.

Presspeech and Wispr Flow compared
Question Presspeech Wispr Flow
Primary workflow Hold hotkey, speak, release; local model transcription is followed by deterministic text handling and normally a paste after confirming the same window is still focused, with manual clipboard recovery when needed. Hold hotkey, speak, and AI-formatted text appears — tone matching, auto-edits, and formatting applied.
Where transcription happens On the Mac. The local Parakeet TDT v3 CoreML model runs on the Apple Neural Engine; no network round-trip for transcription. In the cloud — Wispr's privacy page states "Transcription always happens in the cloud." A separate cloud-storage control governs retained dictation audio, transcripts, and history; it does not make processing local.
Platforms Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 14 or later. Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android, with a shared dictionary across devices.
Price Free and MIT licensed. Free tier of 2,000 words/week on desktop; Pro is $15/month, or $12/month billed annually.
Account and data controls No account, no telemetry, transcript history in memory only. Requires an account. Settings control model-improvement permission, dictation cloud storage, and Notetaker retention; Notetaker transcripts use Wispr's cloud for sync and meeting resumption.
Beyond dictation Deliberately narrow: verbatim transcription, local text corrections, opt-in filler-word removal. AI editing and formatting, tone adjustment, command mode, cross-device personalization, and Notetaker for meetings and voice memos.

Choose Wispr Flow if you want AI-shaped output across many devices, cloud-synced personalization, and meeting notes. Choose Presspeech if you want dictation whose audio never leaves the computer, with no account, cloud service, or recurring cost.
